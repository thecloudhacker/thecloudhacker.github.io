UK Fridges Attacked

3rd November 2026

After the recent issues in European supermarkets with refrigeration units turning into ovens, the British Association of Independent Meat Suppliers (AIMS) announed some of it's over 300 members had unexpectedly become "collateral ".

On Monday, 2nd November, supermarkets in countries including Ireland, Sweden, Estonia, and Belgium reported fridges and freezers being increased in temperature. Sabcobel, the Belgian company who manufactures some of the affected refrigerators, said the evaporators had been switched into defrost mode, causing the units to warm up. The process similar to that seen in France in October.

Many retailers have had to bin spoiled food which is not only a financial loss to the businesses, but a supply issue for the local communities. The ongoing attacks on the food supply industry come at a time when countries across northern Europe are being encouraged to stock several days of supply in case of issues caused by national emergencies (and, implied - due to conflict).

The NCSC have confirmed they have been working alongside British supermarkets to ensure their fridges and freezers are as secure as possible to prevent against these types of attack. The concern is that these are the low-hanging fruit and other critical infrastructure will be next on the list of targets.
