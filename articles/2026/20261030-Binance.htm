Binance Crypto Hack

30th October 2026

Binance, the Chinese Cryptocurrency giant who is touted as the world's biggest crypto exchange just had 4.7 billion pounds (6.3 billion dollars) of digital assets impacted in a hack. Binance have said about half of those assets had been frozen but many remained in the hands of hackers, but adding that it would "cover all losses"

The company has stated that while initial checks suggested an engineer's laptop had been compromised, allowing the attacker to make unauthorised withdrawals, "..subsequent investigation has uncovered additional points of breach".

"To be clear: the attacker did not simply use one engineer's permissions, and this was not a single 'one-click' action. It involved a more complex attack process. Our investigation has not attributed responsibility to any individual. So far, we have found no evidence of any wallet private key leak", said Binance co-CEO, Richard Teng.

Two analytics firms have attributed the incident to a North Korean hacking unit known has TraderTraitor. Chainalysis has also said it has "high confidence" the North Korean group are linked to the hack.

TRM Labs have said in a report that several elements of the Binance attack resemble earlier North Korean-linked incidents, including the Bybit hack. Ongoing updates are expected as Binance continues their investigation.

Two other major crypto platforms, Tether and Circle, said they had frozen a total of about 2.4bn dollars of assets in response to the potential Binance hack and Bitcoin dropped by more than 4% following the alerts with other major cryptocurrencies including Ethereum and Solana also falling sharply.
