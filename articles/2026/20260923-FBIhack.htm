FBI Hack

23rd September 2026

With a tongue-in-cheek "this site has been taken down by ShinyHunters" quip, the roles have been seemingly reversed on the FBI's jobs website.

Claiming their hack of Oracle's PeopleSoft using a zero-day vulnerability, the group have said they stole more than two terrabytes of employee data from the system and their extortion fee for releasing the data? Update the record on how ShinyHunters operates and retract statements made in a May 15th bulletin.

In a shocking twist, the group simply want false claims the group has sensitive or compromising information of victims, that the group uses harassment strategies and sends threateing text messages and phone calls to victims and family members, and in some cases swatting.

ShinyHunters claims none of this is true and they have been trying to combat these allegations.

The zero-day hack on Oracle's PeopleSoft leaves concern that there has been no update from Oracle on acknowleding the issue, or of any critical patches inbound. Given the sensitive information stored in their systems and the wide use in large corporations and government departments, the ability for ShinyHunters (and potentially others) to laterally navigate through systems easily is of great concern.
